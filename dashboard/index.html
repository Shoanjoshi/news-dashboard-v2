T1 K-shaped Economic Recovery OVERVIEW:
Recent analyses highlight a significant divergence in consumer spending trends, whereby high-income households are experiencing faster growth than lower-income households, resulting in a K-shaped economic recovery. This phenomenon is marked by disparities in wealth, inflation effects, and overall spending behaviors between different income segments.

KEY EXAMPLES:
- Aggregate retail spending growth since 2023 has been primarily driven by high-income households, underscoring the K-shaped economy narrative.
- Wealth accumulation among high-income individuals contrasts sharply with rising inflation pressures on low-income households, exacerbating spending discrepancies.
- Increased nominal gasoline spending for high-income households versus decreased real consumption for low-income households further illustrates this K-shaped consumption pattern.
- Businesses reported substantial cost increases that may further influence spending dynamics and inflation expectations, though they currently predict moderating inflation rates.		 40
