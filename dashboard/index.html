T0 ** Income Inequality and Economic Divergence **OVERVIEW:**
Recent U.S. economic growth has been concentrated among high-income households, creating a K-shaped economy where upper earners spend and accumulate wealth faster than lower-income groups. This divergence reflects structural economic shifts including wealth concentration, differential inflation impacts, and evolving labor market dynamics.

**KEY EXAMPLES:**
- Retail spending growth since 2023 has been driven primarily by households earning over $125,000 annually, while lower-income households lag behind
- High-income households experienced greater wealth increases while low-income households faced higher inflation rates, both contributing to spending divergence
- Underlying wage inflation (TWIn) has declined from 2021 peaks but shows signs of stabilizing, with recent decoupling emerging across different sectors		 39
T1 Middle East Crisis and Global Supply Disruptions OVERVIEW:

The disruption comes amid broader shifts in U.S. trade policy with China and potential sanctions relief elsewhere, creating uncertainty about long-term economic realignment.

KEY EXAMPLES:

- Persian Gulf oil producers are developing alternative export routes as they assess regional safety conditions
- U.S. trade deficit with China remained stable at $1.2 trillion despite policy changes, while China's global trade surplus grew to $1.2 trillion and bilateral flows shifted toward southeast Asia		 35

T3 AI's Economic Impact and Adoption OVERVIEW:
Companies are rapidly investing in AI across industries—from accounting to film production—while simultaneously grappling with high capital costs that exceed current operational returns. The technology's effects on labor demand remain mixed, with evidence of workplace disruption emerging alongside ongoing skill development among tech workers.

KEY EXAMPLES:
- Venture-capital firms are deploying AI in unglamorous fields like accounting and property management
- America's largest tech firms spent more on capital investment than they earned from operations in Q3 2025, as AI absorbs resources faster than generating returns
- Job postings for AI-exposed occupations have not shown distinct decline since ChatGPT's release, despite overall hiring slowdown
- Tech workers are spending personal time learning AI tools to remain competitive as the industry rapidly evolves		 33

KEY EXAMPLES:
- Microsoft released nearly 200 security fixes in a single Patch Tuesday cycle, with nearly three dozen rated "critical" and exploit code already publicly available
- A three-stage AI prompt-injection attack using hidden URLs and variables was discovered and patched as part of a new class of LLM security issues
- SearchLeak exploit demonstrates systemic failures in the industry's approach to large language model security
- Fragmented security stacks and manual workarounds increase human errors in identifying vulnerabilities		 25
