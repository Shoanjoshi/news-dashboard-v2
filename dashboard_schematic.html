↓
📥

Article Collection

Hundreds of article summaries are pulled in and cleaned up, ready for analysis.

↓

Topic Clustering (AI)

An AI model automatically groups articles covering the same story or theme — no manual tagging needed.

↓
✨

Claude AI Summarises

For each cluster, Claude reads the articles and writes a concise title and summary of what's happening.

↓
📊

Dashboard Updates

Charts, topic summaries, theme signals and the affinity heatmap are all rebuilt and published live.
