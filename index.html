T0 ** Rising Threats in Cybersecurity Landscape **OVERVIEW:**
Recent cyberattacks demonstrate an escalating threat environment involving state-backed hackers, botnets, AI-enhanced attacks, and social engineering tactics targeting critical infrastructure, educational institutions, and network systems globally. Security vulnerabilities are being discovered and exploited at record rates, while defenders increasingly rely on AI to identify flaws before attackers do.

**KEY EXAMPLES:**
- Russian military intelligence hackers harvested authentication tokens from Microsoft Office users across 18,000+ networks by exploiting known router vulnerabilities
- U.S., Canadian, and German authorities dismantled four major botnets (Aisuru, Kimwolf, JackSkid, Mossad) controlling over 3 million compromised IoT devices responsible for record-breaking DDoS attacks
- A cybercrime group conducted a data extortion attack on Canvas education platform, threatening to leak data from 275 million students across nearly 9,000 institutions
- Two threat campaigns leveraged AI agents to support attacks against targets in Mexico and Brazil		 45

T2 ** Bitcoin Falls Below $80K Amid Inflation Surge **OVERVIEW:**
Bitcoin has declined below the $80,000 level following higher-than-expected US inflation data, including a Producer Price Index (PPI) that hit 6%—matching 2022 levels. The stronger-than-anticipated inflation readings have revived concerns about prolonged higher interest rates, offsetting earlier expectations for Federal Reserve rate cuts and creating headwinds for crypto markets.

**KEY EXAMPLES:**
- Bitcoin price slipped from the low $81,000s to $79,706 following April's hotter-than-expected US inflation print, with $80,000 becoming a key intraday support level
- April headline CPI rose 3.8% year-over-year (above the expected 3.7%), putting rate hikes back on the table instead of the rate cuts crypto markets had anticipated
- US PPI inflation hit 6% in April, the highest level since 2022, compounding inflation concerns alongside geopolitical tensions including US-Iran conflict and elevated oil prices
- Trump's visit to Beijing and tariff threats added additional market uncertainty during an already volatile macro week for Bitcoin trading		 43
T3 K-Shaped Economic Divergence and Pressures OVERVIEW:
Recent economic data reveals a widening gap between high-income and low-income households since 2023, with consumer spending and wealth gains concentrated among higher earners while lower-income groups face greater inflation pressures. This K-shaped pattern reflects structural economic shifts affecting wages, prices, and macroeconomic stability.

KEY EXAMPLES:
- Retail spending growth since 2023 has been driven primarily by high-income households earning over $125,000 annually, while lower-income households lag behind
- In March 2026, energy price surges created divergent gasoline spending patterns: high-income households maintained real consumption while low-income households reduced consumption despite higher nominal spending
- Wealth has increased most for high-income households since 2023, while inflation has risen most for low-income households, exacerbating the divergence in real spending power		 42

T8 Federal Reserve Policy and Banking Sector Stress OVERVIEW:
Recent developments in monetary policy, regulatory changes, and financial sector stress are reshaping the banking landscape. The Federal Reserve is managing economic conditions through multiple policy tools while policymakers monitor emerging risks from nonbank financial institutions and broader market uncertainties.

KEY EXAMPLES:
- The Fed's 2022-23 tightening cycle used both administrative rate changes and balance sheet adjustments to influence money market financing conditions
- Nonbank financial institution (NBFI) bankruptcies and fund wind-downs, including Tricolor, First Brands, and Blue Owl Capital Corp II, have directly impacted bank stocks despite assessments that systemic risk remains limited
- Kevin Warsh was confirmed as Federal Reserve chairman amid ongoing concerns about central bank independence and inflation at three-year highs
- Regulators are modernizing capital frameworks and enhancing leverage ratios for community banks to strengthen the banking system
- The Senate Banking Committee's CLARITY Act cryptocurrency bill faces over 100 amendments, reflecting tensions between banks, Democrats, and crypto industry groups over stablecoin regulation		 23

T10 Iran Conflict Drives Oil and Inflation Surge OVERVIEW:

The supply disruption has resulted in an estimated loss of nearly 1 billion barrels over 75 days, with consequences rippling through consumer prices.

KEY EXAMPLES:
- Gasoline prices surged, pushing April inflation to its highest level since late 2023
- Grocery store prices have increased as effects of the war ripple through the economy
- Airline ticket prices are rising due to sustained high oil prices from the US and Israel's conflict with Iran
- US inflation reached its highest level since May 2023 as consumers feel the broader economic impact		 21
